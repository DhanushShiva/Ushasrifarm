A farm built around freshness

USHASRI Farms grows oyster mushrooms the natural way, with attention at every stage from spawn to harvest. Our focus is simple: tender, fresh mushrooms of consistent quality.

Alongside mushrooms, we supply mushroom spawn for people who want to start their own cultivation, and pure Nati Koli, the native country chicken. You deal directly with the farm, and that trust is what we work to earn.
